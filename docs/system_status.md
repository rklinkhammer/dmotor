## Next implementation and validation

Follow the [ordered one-board sequence](system_bringup_plan.md#current-one-board-sequence--2026-11-12):

1. Record the current Zybo/ESP32 images and assembled-board population; inspect
   power, sense connections and separate 3V3 harness supplies. Keep drive inhibited.
2. Complete assembled-fixture IO validation: UART on PS UART0, HB loss/latch/rearm,
   SW0, STOP/HALT/watchdog/permit outputs, and manual encoder count/direction.
   Preserve the already-passed `hb10` detection result.
3. Validate the actual single INA address and ADC calibration, then implement or
   configure an explicit one-board inhibited workload. Absent boards must not be
   spoofed as healthy or silently removed from a four-board configuration.
4. Complete the operational safety/recovery and measured deadline work for this
   restricted population. Only then enter the existing bounded single-motor powered
   procedure after ITRIP verification and explicit gate acceptance.

D10 accepts motor damage from slow overload monitoring **conditional on verified
ITRIP**. It does not waive STOP/HALT, battery/wiring limits, invalid-data inhibition
or no-unintended-restart requirements. Monitor cadence remains 16 ms, <12 ms work
and >=4 ms reserve. Daemon clients use `dmotor_client`; no automatic/live recovery
is assumed. Four-device timing and multi-motor power interactions remain future
expansion tests, not prerequisites to unrelated inhibited one-board measurements.
